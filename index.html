How it works

Build your inventory in three simple steps.

  1. Take photos

    Photograph your belongings, or walk through a room with Room Sweep and capture one item after another.

  2. Add details

    Add receipts, values, warranty dates and notes. Scan a barcode to look up many products.

  3. Stay organized

    Sort by property, room and category, and export a PDF report, a spreadsheet or a full backup.

Legacy Planning PRO

Decide who receives what.

Some things matter more than their price. Legacy Planning lets you write down who should have them, so your family isn’t left guessing.

  • Add the people in your life, with how they’re related to you.
  • Choose a recipient for an item, or set a rule for a whole room or category.
  • Export an Estate Distribution PDF listing who receives what, with values, to share with family or your executor.

Legacy Planning is an organizational record of your wishes. It is not a will or a legal document, and does not replace advice from a lawyer.

Your privacy matters

Your data. On your device.

Odune keeps your inventory on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing leaves your device unless you export or back it up yourself. The one optional network request is a barcode lookup, which sends only the barcode’s digits.
