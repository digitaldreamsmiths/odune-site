01Catalog

Document a whole room in minutes, not weekends.

The reason most people don't have a home inventory is that making one is tedious. Odune is built to remove every tap it can.

  • Room Sweep — keep the camera open and snap item after item; each photo becomes a draft entry automatically.
  • On-device recognition suggests a name and category for every photo — pictures never leave your phone.
  • Barcode autofill — scan a product barcode to fill in name, brand, and category.
  • One-tap templates for common rooms and household items.
02Insure

Ready for the day you hope never comes.

A burst pipe, a break-in, a fire. When the adjuster asks what you lost, the answer shouldn't live in your memory.

  • Everything a claim needs — photos, receipts, serial numbers, purchase price, and estimated value per item.
  • Value at a glance — per room, per property, and in total.
  • Export freely — a formatted PDF report, a CSV spreadsheet, or a complete backup with photos. On the free plan, always.
  • Warranty reminders — optional alerts 30 days before a warranty runs out.
03Pass it on

Legacy PlanningPRO

Some things in your home are destined for someone. Odune's optional Legacy Planning module lets you record who receives what — whole categories, rooms, or properties, with per-item exceptions and personal notes — and export an Estate Distribution PDF to keep with your will.

It's invisible until you turn it on, and it stays on your device like everything else. The Estate Distribution PDF is an organizational record for your family and executor — it is not a will or legal instrument.

05Privacy

What we collect: nothing.

Odune has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Photo recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks. The only optional network request the app can make is a barcode lookup — and it sends the barcode digits, nothing else.
